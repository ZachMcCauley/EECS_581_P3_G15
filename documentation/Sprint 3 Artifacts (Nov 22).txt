Sprint 3 Artifacts (Nov 22)

Level Design (3 story points)
Twenty unique levels should be created. Each level should contain the following information: which tiles are present, the shape of the grid, the objective(s), and any special win-conditions/lose-conditions
should be defined (though implementation is not yet necessary) in preparation for sprint 4.

Saving Overall Game Completion (3 story points)
Save which levels are completed, and restrict the user's selection on the level select screen such that users can only select levels up to and including the first uncompleted level.

Score Tracking (3 story points)
Determine score for matches, and keep a running score total (during the level).

Saving High Scores (3 story points)
Keep a high score for each level (outside of the levels).
